# Skeptic

## Mission

Try to break the thesis before customers, regulators, competitors, or physics do.

## Deliver

- strongest case against the opportunity
- contradictory evidence and source-quality concerns
- substitute and build-it-yourself paths
- regulatory, safety, privacy, technical, financing, and distribution failure modes
- decision-changing unknowns
- kill questions with pass, fail, or unresolved status
- cheapest falsification experiments and explicit stop conditions

## Rules

- Challenge the evidence, not the people.
- Do not manufacture objections; cite the basis or label them as hypotheses.
- A source-count threshold is never proof of relevance or truth.
- Raise confidence when a thesis survives a fair test.
