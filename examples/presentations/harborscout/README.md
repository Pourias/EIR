# HarborScout historical draft deck

This 18-slide investor deck is preserved as a **historical draft and presentation-workflow example**, not as a send-ready pitch. It shows how EIR translated a startup thesis into a visual narrative while keeping unknown company facts visible.

## What it teaches

- a seed-deck spine from problem through ask
- product-system and mission-flow diagrams
- separation of market signals from company traction
- explicit assumptions where founder, prototype, customer, and fundraising facts were missing
- the value of rendering and reviewing every slide before external use

## Known QA limitations

- Slide 3 has a title/body overlap. It is retained so the source and exported artifacts remain an honest historical set.
- Slides 13, 14, and 18 contain explicit team, raise, traction, prototype, and customer placeholders.
- The PPTX expects Aptos fonts and does not embed them; another office suite may substitute fonts and change line wrapping.
- The PDF is a raster export, so it is not searchable or accessible like a tagged, text-based PDF.
- Slide 18 refers to a private planning map that is not published. The related public concept memo is the [micro-USV diligence example](../../diligence/2026-05-26_micro-ai-unmanned-surface-vessel-diligence-memo.md).

Before external use, fix the overlap, replace every placeholder with verified company facts, add direct source links, embed or replace fonts, and regenerate an accessible PDF.
